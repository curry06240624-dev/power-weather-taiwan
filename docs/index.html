Methodology

Data

  • Taipower open data: daily reserve and reserve rate. Peak = reserve ÷ rate, for 1,413 days (1 Jan 2023 to 13 Nov 2026). We found 16 typos in Taipower's full table and fixed or avoided them.
  • Holidays: the government office calendar (DGPA). The whole Lunar New Year break is marked.
  • Weather: Open-Meteo for seven cities, weighted by population. Training uses the weather that happened; the 2026 test uses the forecast made the day before.

Test

Split by time: train on 2023–2024, choose settings on 2025, test once on 1 January to 13 November 2026 (317 days).
